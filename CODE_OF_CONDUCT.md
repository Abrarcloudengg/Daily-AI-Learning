# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming community, we pledge to make participation in Daily AI Learning a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.

Examples of unacceptable behavior by participants:

- Harassment, discrimination, or hateful speech.
- Personal attacks or insults.
- Publishing private information without consent.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainers.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take action to keep the community safe and productive.
